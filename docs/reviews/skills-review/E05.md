# E05 Voice Engineer: skills review (2026-10-18)

## Role summary
E05 owns `packages/voice`: the audio worker pipeline from wake to VAD, STT, TTS and barge-in, plus device selection and the privacy/egress guard. Tickets: DOT-006 MDC reuse check (with E01), DOT-020 audio runtime/wake spike (with E10), ★DOT-021 audio worker with zero egress, DOT-022 TTS/barge-in/echo (with E10). M1 per voice-plan §10. Targets (spec §14): wake→listening ≤500 ms p95, interrupt-to-stop ≤300 ms p95, zero standby egress, mute closes the stream, one UtteranceReady per requestId, and speech billing kept separate from ChatGPT.

## Existing coverage
- dottie-audio-capture-dsp-check: EchoGate sweep, mute, pktmon egress, device matrix
- dottie-wake-word-far-frr
- dottie-voice-latency-eval
- dottie-e2e-voice-action-suite

## Gaps found
1. The STT/TTS decision (Q4) has no method. There's no cost meter recipe, no price re-verification, and key handling is open. GPT-Live is still named in the E05 profile even though voice-plan rules it out of V1.
2. Vendoring MDC has no repeatable recipe. That covers verbatim copy, hashes, the 85-test port and the shim. MDC has no LICENSE, and the mirror exists now (`e10/mdc_mirror`), which changes voice-plan's "not on the box".
3. Utterance and barge-in semantics have no test checklist. endReason is spelled three different ways.
4. The worker↔main RPC doesn't match the desktop `VoiceService` port. Examples: `setMute` vs `setMicSuppressed`, and there are no VoiceState/SpeechCost events. Mute and egress have to be enforced in the worker.
