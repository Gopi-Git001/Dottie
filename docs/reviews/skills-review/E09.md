# E09 Perf & Reliability Engineer: skills review (2026-10-18)

## Role summary
E09 owns `packages/observability` and supervision policy.
- **Instrumentation:** QPC clock, PerfMark/ResourceSample/LifecycleEvent, W/I spans (perf-reliability-plan §1).
- **DOT-042:** instrumentation, the 8 h soak, and the eager vs lazy app-server decision.
- **Policy:** standby budgets (all PROVISIONAL), the power/device matrix, the 7-day soak (M6), and the supervisor policy (ADR-0005).
- **Targets:** W ≤500 ms p95 and I ≤300 ms p95 (n≥200 per stratum), bounded standby resources, no restart loops, no orphans.

## Existing coverage
- dottie-voice-latency-eval: W/I measurement, nearest-rank, UCB, strata.
- dottie-soak-and-crash-triage: soak collectors, crash triage, breaker check.
- dottie-evidence-pack.
- dottie-weekly-status-report.

## Gaps found
1. No rules exist yet for emitting marks, the sink, powerEpoch/bootId invalidation, or the 10k ping-pong clock calibration. The contract gaps (WakeDetected clock/correlationId) are unproposed.
2. No standby-budget procedure covers: a Dottie-off baseline, the logman/powercfg set, ABBA battery, or the eager-vs-lazy comparison including the first-task penalty.
3. No run sheet for the power/device matrix. Unlock is manual, Modern Standby events are unverified, and it is unknown whether Gopi's PC is a laptop.
4. The supervisor policy conflicts across three places:
   - ADR-0005 and `supervisor.ts`: 1 s→60 s, no jitter, terminal `gave_up`, env failures counted as crashes.
   - Perf plan: full jitter, 500 ms/30 s/120 s, half-open breaker, envUnavailable.
   - Coordinator retry is a separate layer.
